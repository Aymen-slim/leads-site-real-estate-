# Surface Brief: Norway House & Land Seller Platform

## Mode
Persuade (Landing Page) + Operate (5 Zero-Typing Calculators & 4-Tap Seller Funnel)

## Audience & Job
Norwegian property owners aged 30–55 (with primary ergonomic focus on 45+ homeowners and landowners) looking to sell a residential house/villa, apartment, or constructible plot of land. They want immediate clarity on neighborhood price per m², net cash after taxes/fees, land subdivision potential, family inheritance payout, or sell-as-is vs. renovation tradeoffs—without typing on small mobile keyboards or dragging maps.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A Scandinavian real-estate valuation and seller-acquisition platform modeled on Krogsveen ("Den grønne kjeden") that replaces multi-field brokerage forms and noisy buyer portals with oversized, zero-typing interactive calculators and a 4-tap auto-advancing seller funnel.

OWN-WORLD: Committed Krogsveen forest green (`#0B5335`) and vibrant action green (`#167A4B`) over warm Nordic alabaster (`#F6F8F6`) and crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) surfaces, deep charcoal ink (`#0F1A14`), 2px structural borders (`#C9D6CE`), Albert Sans display typography paired with Atkinson Hyperlegible tabular numerals, and uniform 1.75px stroke custom architectural SVG icons (zero emojis).

STORY: The homeowner immediately sees live local Norwegian market figures (NOK/m², net payout, or buildability), adjusts a single oversized control with zero typing, and taps a tailored green button that pre-fills their property context into a 4-tap phone-only seller funnel.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top Krogsveen-green utility and navigation header with instant Norwegian/English toggle and A/B test switcher; full-width two-column architectural split where the left column delivers the headline and immediate neighborhood price-per-m² lookup (Variant A) or Step-1 giant property-type tap cards (Variant B), and the right column presents a live interactive valuation ledger card.
